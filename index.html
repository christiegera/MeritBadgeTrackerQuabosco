How each badge works

  • Before you start a badge: talk to your Scoutmaster and get the blue card signed (or recorded in Scoutbook).
  • Do each requirement exactly as written. Mom can't change or skip any, even though she's your counselor.
  • When you finish one, tap it to Ready for review and add a note about what you did and where the proof is.
  • Mom reviews it with you and marks it Signed off.

Before you post anything

  • Got the Legion's OK to film? The camp is private property.
  • Anyone recognizable in the shot agreed to be posted (and their parent, if they're a kid)?
  • Location tags turned off on the photo?
  • Music is licensed or royalty-free?
  • Website or blog going live? Mom approves it first. Communication 7 and Digital Technology 6 require it.
